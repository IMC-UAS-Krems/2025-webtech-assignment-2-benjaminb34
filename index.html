Sheltered Dogs Foundation
Our organization’s main goal is to provide essential items to shelters with limited resources and insufficient equipment to properly care for their animals. We act as a bridge between citizens and shelters: the equipment ordered through our platform is delivered directly by us to the shelters in need. We believe every dog deserves proper care, comfort, and support.
Our organization works with 23 different shelters
  • Proivde medicine, food, and other essentials
  • Provide emergency care for dogs in crisis
  • Partnering with volunteers
  • Improving living conditions
