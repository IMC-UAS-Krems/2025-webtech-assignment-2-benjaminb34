Open Table Foundation
Our organization’s main goal is to provide essential items to homeless shelters with limited resources and insufficient equipment to properly care for their occupants. We act as a bridge between citizens and homeless shelters: the equipment ordered through our platform is delivered directly by us to the shelters in need. We believe every person deserves proper care, comfort, and support.
Our organization works with 23 different shelters
  • Proivde medicine, food, and other essentials
  • Provide emergency care for people in crisis
  • Partnering with volunteers
  • Improving living conditions
